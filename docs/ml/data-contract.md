## 3. Scientific Decision on Surface Barometric Pressure (Option A - Recommended)

* **Decision**: Surface pressure is **removed from the V1 heat-risk feature set**.
* **Scientific Rationale**: Atmospheric pressure across low-elevation Indian urban plains provides negligible marginal predictive value for short-range heatwave escalation relative to ambient temperature, humidity, apparent temperature, and wet-bulb temperature.
* **Integrity Guarantee**: Missing pressure measurements are **never** imputed to standard sea-level pressure ($1013.25\text{ hPa}$). Imputing an unobserved field with a constant creates false signals for tree-based split criteria. Raw observation schemas preserve `surface_pressure_hpa = None` when unobserved.

## 4. Target Contract ($y$)

* **Target Name**: `target_heat_risk_category`
* **Forecast Horizon**: $H = 6$ Hours
* **Target Interval**: $(t, t + 6\text{h}]$ (Strictly forward; never includes $t$ or earlier)
* **Categories**:
  * `LOW`: Forward $\max(HI) < 32^\circ\text{C} \land \max(T_w) < 25^\circ\text{C}$
  * `MODERATE`: Forward $\max(HI) \in [32^\circ\text{C}, 41^\circ\text{C}) \lor \max(T_w) \ge 25^\circ\text{C}$
  * `HIGH`: Forward $\max(HI) \in [41^\circ\text{C}, 46^\circ\text{C}) \lor \max(T_w) \ge 27.5^\circ\text{C}$
  * `SEVERE`: Forward $\max(HI) \in [46^\circ\text{C}, 54^\circ\text{C}) \lor \max(T_w) \ge 29^\circ\text{C}$
  * `CRITICAL`: Forward $\max(HI) \ge 54^\circ\text{C} \lor \max(T_w) \ge 31^\circ\text{C}$

## 5. Temporal Partitioning & Leakage Rules

1. **Chronological Splitting Only**:
   * Oldest 70% $\rightarrow$ Train (`2023-01-01T00:00:00Z` to `2024-05-26T15:00:00Z`)
   * Middle 15% $\rightarrow$ Validation (`2024-05-26T16:00:00Z` to `2024-09-13T06:00:00Z`)
   * Most recent 15% $\rightarrow$ Test (`2024-09-13T07:00:00Z` to `2024-12-31T22:00:00Z`)
2. **Boundary Isolation Enforced**:
   $$\max(\text{train\_timestamp}) < \min(\text{val\_timestamp})$$
   $$\max(\text{val\_timestamp}) < \min(\text{test\_timestamp})$$
3. **Multi-Location Time Synchronization**:
   Splits are partitioned on **unique timestamps**, ensuring that all 10 locations observed at hour $t$ remain in the same partition without boundary collision.
